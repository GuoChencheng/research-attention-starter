# Worked example: are all primes odd?

## Objective
Determine whether every prime is odd, using the usual definition for positive integers.

## First attempt: finite evidence
3, 5, and 7 are odd primes. Status: exact_finite. These observations do not establish the universal claim.

## Counterexample
2 is prime and even. Status: refuted for the original claim. Preserve the finite checks and explain why they were insufficient.

## Revised claim
Every prime greater than 2 is odd. An even integer greater than 2 has the proper divisor 2 and is therefore not prime. Status: proved under the stated definition and scope.

## Reusable summary
Small successful examples do not prove a universal statement. One valid counterexample refutes it. A revised statement needs its own argument. The original failure remains part of the record.
